## On the adaptations

Skills in `skills/` are not verbatim copies. They have been ported from their upstreams to Claude Code and Antigravity formats, normalized for frontmatter and link integrity, and integrated into the `flow` router. Where a capability could not be ported honestly, it was parked rather than left as instructions that would fail on first use. See `parked/README.md`.

None of the upstream authors have reviewed or endorsed this derivative.
